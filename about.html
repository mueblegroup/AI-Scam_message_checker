About the project

A practical second opinion for suspicious messages.

ScamShield AI helps Malaysian users recognise phishing, impersonation, unsolicited loan promotions and other common scam patterns.

How it works

The checker combines multiple warning signs rather than relying on one keyword.

  • Looks for urgency, threats, payment pressure and suspicious links.
  • Recognises deliveries, bank alerts, task jobs, loans and investments.
  • Explains the matched warning signs and a safer next step.

Privacy and limitations

Analysis runs locally in the browser. Messages are not uploaded by the static website.

Important: A low score is not a safety guarantee. Verify through an independently found official channel.
