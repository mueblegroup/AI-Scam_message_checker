About the project

A practical second opinion for suspicious messages.

ScamShield AI is an educational, browser-based checker built to help Malaysian users recognise phishing, impersonation, unsolicited loan promotions and other common scam patterns before they click, reply or pay.

How it works

The checker combines multiple warning signs instead of relying on a single keyword.

01
Behavioural patternsLooks for urgency, threats, payment pressure, secrecy, reply hooks and suspicious links.
02
Message contextRecognises common themes such as fake deliveries, bank alerts, task jobs, loans and investment pitches.
03
Clear explanationShows which warning signs were found and recommends a safer next step.

Privacy and limitations

Analysis currently runs locally in the browser. Messages are not uploaded or stored by the static website.

Important: This tool cannot prove that a sender is genuine or fraudulent. A low score is not a safety guarantee. Always verify through an independently found official website, app or phone number.
