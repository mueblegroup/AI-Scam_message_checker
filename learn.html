Scam awareness guide

Recognise the hook before it becomes a loss.

Most scam messages try to make you react quickly. Slow down and verify every unexpected request independently.

Links and account alerts

  • “Verify your account” or “payment failed”
  • Shortened or misspelled domains
  • Threats of account suspension
  • APK or remote-access app requests

Reply and conversation hooks

  • “Reply YES/NO,” “if keen,” or “text back”
  • Wrong-number messages becoming overly friendly
  • Unexpected loan, job or investment introductions
  • Requests to move to another platform

Money and reward pressure

  • Parcel, customs, toll or redelivery fees
  • Guaranteed returns and easy task income
  • Prizes, refunds and free gifts
  • Upfront deposits or processing charges

Impersonation and secrets

  • Bank, police, tax authority or courier claims
  • A boss or relative using a “new number”
  • OTP, TAC, PIN, CVV or password requests
  • Instructions not to tell anyone

What to do

Pause. Do not click, reply or pay while pressured. Verify independently. Open the official app yourself or use a published contact. Block and report. Preserve evidence and report the sender.
